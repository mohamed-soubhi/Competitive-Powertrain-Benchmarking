Understanding EU HDV Emissions Standards & Test Cycles

1. Engine Test-Cycle Emissions (Bench Approval)

Under Euro VI (Reg. 595/2009), the engine alone is evaluated on a dynamometer independently of vehicle chassis aerodynamics:

  • WHTC (World Harmonised Transient Cycle): Dynamometer speed and torque continuously vary over 1800 seconds, including urban, rural, and highway phases. Unit: g/kWh.
  • WHSC (World Harmonised Steady-State Cycle): 13 discrete steady operating speed/load points. Unit: g/kWh.

2. Whole-Vehicle VECTO Simulation (CO2v g/km)

The European Commission’s Vehicle Energy Consumption Calculation Tool (VECTO) simulates whole-vehicle emissions taking into account:

  • Engine fuel consumption map and friction
  • Gearbox transmission loss and axle ratios
  • Air drag resistance (Cd × A) and tyre rolling resistance coefficients (RRC)
  • Auxiliary electrical / mechanical loads (cooling fan, steering pump, pneumatic compressor)

3. Freight Transport Efficiency (g/t-km)

Calculates grams of CO2 emitted per metric tonne of payload hauled per kilometer (COL_CO2_gtkm), representing true logistics utility and freight efficiency.
